R, the risk unit

R is what you planned to lose if the stop was hit: the distance from entry to stop, times the size, times what one unit of price move is worth. That is 1 for shares and coins; for forex lots it is the pip value per lot, and for contracts the value per point.

This trade: (121.20 − 118.20) × 20 × 1 = 60.00 = 1R. It made 120.00, which is +2.00R.
